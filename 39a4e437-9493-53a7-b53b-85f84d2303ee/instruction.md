# Multiphase CICD carry repair

This workspace runs a five phase delivery pipeline (lint, build, test, package, publish) through a trusted engine named cicd that lives outside your repair surface. Each phase threads one record onto an accumulating carry at /workspace/state/carry.json, and every record names how many earlier phases it inherited through its carried count, so a healthy carry has the record at index i reporting a carried count of exactly i. The engine can also emit a report through cicd report, and it calls the pipeline complete only when all five phases are present in canonical order, the digest chain links end to end, and each record carries its own index.

The operating contract at /workspace/pipeline/carry.spec fixes the canonical phase order and the carried invariant, and it is not part of your repair surface. During a recent incident window the package phase was isolated onto its own carry inside the workspace driver at /workspace/bin/run-pipeline so a flaky publisher could not observe the earlier phases, and that isolation folds the accumulated carry down to the test tip before packaging and then copies the isolated carry back over the pipeline carry. The result is that lint and build silently drop out of the recorded history, the package and publish records are threaded against a truncated carry, and the engine no longer reports a complete pipeline even though every phase ran.

The engine is trusted and correct; the fault is entirely in how the driver hands the long horizon carry across phases. Repair /workspace/bin/run-pipeline so the full accumulated carry survives every phase, the package phase is threaded against the complete history rather than a folded snapshot, and cicd report declares the pipeline complete with five records and a final carried count of four. You may edit files under /workspace/bin and state under /workspace/state. You must not edit /workspace/pipeline/carry.spec, and you must not hand write or fabricate a carry or a report; the pipeline is only complete when the engine itself threads it end to end.
