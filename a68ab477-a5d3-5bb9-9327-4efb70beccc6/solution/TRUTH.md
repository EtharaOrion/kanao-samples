# Ground truth: pipeline-state-collapse

The trusted engine at /usr/local/lib/tfmock/tfmock.py threads a five tier reconcile (network, iam, compute, data, dns) onto an accumulating ledger, where each record carries a count equal to its index, the digest chain links every apply end to end, and every record carries an HMAC seal produced from a key generated at environment start under /usr/local/lib/tfmock/seal.key. The engine is immutable and lives outside the repair surface; only the workspace driver at /workspace/bin/run-reconcile is editable.

The planted fault is entirely in the driver. Before the data tier apply the driver runs tfmock snapshot with keep set to one, folding the accumulated ledger down to the compute tip, then applies the data tier against that isolated one record ledger and copies it back over the reconcile ledger. This collapses the long horizon state: the network and iam records drop out of the recorded history, so the drift those tiers corrected silently reappears, and the data and dns records are threaded against a truncated ledger. The engine then reports complete false because the ledger no longer holds five in order records with matching carried counts.

The repair threads the full accumulating ledger through every apply. The reference solution rewrites the driver to apply all five tiers in order against the single reconcile ledger, drops the snapshot fold entirely, then runs tfmock verify and tfmock report. The engine threads five sealed records with carried counts zero through four, verify passes, and report declares the reconcile complete with a final carried count of four, yielding reward 1.0000.

The fabrication guard is the engine seal, not the mere shape of the ledger. A solver that hand writes a five record ledger can copy the public digest chain but cannot forge the per record HMAC seal, so tfmock audit reports forged true and the fabrication detector fires. An honest but incomplete repair that still lets the engine write its records keeps valid seals and is never flagged as fabrication; it simply loses the positive threading and completeness credit until the full ledger survives. This keeps the fabrication penalty off honest mistakes while still blocking a hand written answer.
